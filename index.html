Privacy Policy for NCE Exam Prep: CUE

Publisher: Lumidev · Android package: app.cue.nceprep
Effective date: November 5, 2026

Current closed-test version

This version uses Google demo advertisements. Demo ads do not generate publisher advertising revenue. Your study answers and progress are stored locally by CUE, but the Google advertising SDK communicates with Google and may process device and usage information.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy describes the CUE Android app and this privacy page. CUE is an independent study aid for adults preparing for the National Counselor Examination. It does not provide patient care, diagnosis, treatment or an assessment of your personal mental health. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by NBCC.

For support and privacy questions, contact lumidev.support@gmail.com. This support mailbox is shared with our other apps; identify CUE in your message.

2. Study records on your device

CUE stores your practice responses, confidence selections, saved questions, session history, active session, exam-date preference and settings on your device. The app does not require a CUE account. We do not operate a server receiving this study record, a CUE cloud-sync service or a separate developer analytics service.

Optional bonus credits and advertising frequency records are stored separately from the study backup. CUE does not ask you to submit a personal clinical history, patient record or diagnostic information.

3. Google advertising and technical data

The Android app includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Current ad requests use Google's sample App ID and sample ad units for banner, app-open, interstitial and optional rewarded formats.

Google's SDK documentation describes collection and sharing of IP addresses, which may indicate approximate location; app and advertisement interactions, such as launches, taps and video views; diagnostic and performance information; and device or account identifiers, including advertising and app-set identifiers when available. Google describes purposes including advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, with SDK data encrypted in transit using TLS.

Study questions work offline. Advertising requires connectivity. CUE does not request precise location, contacts, camera or microphone access for its learning features. Demo advertising does not mean that SDK network processing is absent.

Read Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy. Android settings provide controls for the advertising ID. Live advertising is not enabled in this build; any future activation requires corresponding configuration, applicable privacy choices and updated disclosures.

4. Backups and your choices

You can export your study record as a JSON backup from Settings. Android lets you choose the destination. The file may contain your answers, dates and progress. If you choose cloud storage or share a file, that destination's practices apply; CUE does not upload it to a developer-operated backup server.

Importing a backup asks for confirmation before replacing the current study record. Advertising bonus credits are not included in the study backup. Keep exported files only in places you trust.

5. Support messages and external services

If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to send so we can answer your request. Please avoid sending patient information, passwords or other unnecessary sensitive material. The mailbox provider's practices are described in Google's privacy notice.

Reference-reading links open external websites with their own privacy practices. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose hosting and request handling are described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. This page includes no custom analytics, advertising, remote fonts or tracking scripts.

6. Retention, deletion and requests

Your local study record remains until you reset study progress, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Resetting study progress does not remove separately stored ad records; clearing app storage removes both local stores. Backups saved elsewhere are separate copies and must be removed from those locations.

We keep support communications as needed to handle the request and relevant obligations. You can contact us about access, correction or deletion of information you have sent us. We cannot identify or delete a local study record through a CUE server because no such study-record server is operated. Google's and other providers' own controls may also be needed for information they hold.

Depending on your location, applicable privacy rights and complaint routes may be available. Contact us with the application name and the nature of your request; do not send sensitive identification documents unless an appropriate need and method have been established.

7. Audience and updates

CUE is intended for adult learners and is not designed as a children's service. The current question bank is educational development content with independent counselor review pending. Practice results do not establish exam readiness.

We will update this page and its effective date when the app's relevant processing changes. Before enabling live ads or new data-handling features, the privacy notice and Google Play disclosures must reflect the actual implementation.
